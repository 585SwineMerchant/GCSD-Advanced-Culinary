# Advanced Culinary Field Manual Restructure Rationale

## What Made the Culinary Arts 1 & 2 Manual Successful

The Culinary Arts 1 & 2 site succeeds because its interface and instructional design use the same organizing logic.

1. **The course structure is visible.** Students see eight connected units and can return to any one without losing the overall sequence.
2. **The current location is obvious.** The home page identifies the current unit, progress, and next action.
3. **The unit is the repeated reading pattern.** Each unit contains its overview, lessons, preparation, production connection, response, progress, and connected recipes.
4. **Information is revealed when it becomes useful.** The home page does not show every lesson, recipe, definition, and reference at once.
5. **Primary work and lookup work are separated.** Units organize learning; recipes and quick references support it without becoming competing course sequences.
6. **The language tells students why before asking them how.** Purpose, professional connection, success criteria, and next steps frame the work.
7. **The manual supports before, during, and after lab.** Students can prepare, perform, evaluate, and preserve evidence in one connected environment.
8. **Repeated visual and interaction patterns reduce explanation.** Once a student understands one unit, the rest of the site behaves predictably.

The central success is not the quantity of accumulated content. It is that the site makes the course feel understandable.

## Translation to Advanced Culinary

Advanced Culinary requires a different repeated unit. A textbook chapter or culinary topic cannot serve as the primary container because authentic events create the course rhythm.

The Advanced equivalent of the Intro manual’s repeated unit is one complete event cycle:

1. Brief
2. Learn
3. Plan
4. Produce
5. Close
6. Improve

The same cycle repeats across all six major experiences. Menu, client, quantity, technique, and operating conditions change, but the student never has to relearn how to use the manual.

## Governing Information Architecture

- **Home answers:** What are we producing? Where are we in the cycle? What do I do next?
- **Event Workspace organizes:** the complete job from accepted commitment through closeout and improvement.
- **Connected Learning answers:** What do we need to learn because of this menu or production problem?
- **Recipe Studio answers:** How do we move from a plausible idea to one tested, approved production version?
- **Quick Reference answers:** What operating protocol applies right now?
- **Contextual tools answer:** What document must be completed at this phase?

## Important Boundaries

- Events are the course structure; ProStart Second Edition is the information source.
- Culinary deep dives occur because the event creates a need.
- Advanced Culinary students encounter operating and entrepreneurial concepts through authentic work.
- Kitchen Management owns detailed analysis and uses verified event data to brief the next cycle.
- Management students evaluate the operation, not the cooks.
- Adult planning questions do not appear as student navigation.
- Closeout is part of production, not leftover teacher work.
- Honest failure can be diagnosed and corrected; it is not erased.
- Once the department accepts a client commitment, the client does not absorb the consequences of instructional failure.
