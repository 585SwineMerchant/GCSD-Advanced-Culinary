How performance is treated

Failure has consequences. Honest recovery remains learning.

The original technical result remains visible in the assessment. Recognition, diagnosis, timely communication, compliance with instructor direction, and corrective action are separate evidence of professional recovery.

Unsafe food, a critical safety violation, concealment, knowingly serving a failed product, or an unauthorized correction may require an immediate stop, restart, intervention, or targeted reassessment.
