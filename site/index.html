Phase 1

Understand the brief

Teacher-framed launch

The performance challenge

Produce and serve a safe, consistent, visually appealing small-bite reception that is fully ready at the promised service time.

Recipient and service

The exact opening audience, event date, menu limits, and service format will be confirmed by the teacher. The same planning and evidence process works for a Cottage event, pathway showcase, invited-staff reception, or approved equivalent.

What success looks like

  • Safe food and professional work habits throughout.
  • Uniform portion, flavor, texture, and presentation.
  • Station ready before production begins.
  • Status and risks communicated early.
  • Meaningful cooking completed by every student.
  • Individual evidence preserved after service.
Individual evidenceReadiness check · production contribution · reflection
